我把每一個會全螢幕出現的畫面都走過一次。**目前只有主選單有背景圖**，其餘
全是純黑。以下按「在攤位上會被看到幾次」排序。

收進遊戲一律：

```bash

```

一輪七夜就是各看七次。

## 共用規格（三張都一樣）

- **1800 × 1300、橫的、不透明、鋪滿**
- 這是**背景**，字會壓在上面 —— 所以要**很暗、很低對比**
- **中間三分之二要留空**（那裡是面板和按鈕）
- 不要有主體、不要有文字、不要有邊框

---

## 1️⃣ 七夜地圖 · `title.map`

```
Pixel-art background illustration, 1800x1300, opaque, 16-bit SNES style.
Hard pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, limited palette.

A moonlit valley seen from a high vantage point: rolling black pine forest
filling the lower half, a winding pale dirt road threading from the bottom left
toward the upper right, distant hills fading into haze, a thin crescent moon and
scattered stars in a deep violet sky.

VERY dark and low contrast — this sits behind a map of glowing nodes and text,
so it must never compete with them. The middle two thirds of the frame should be
the emptiest, darkest part; keep the detail at the edges.

No characters, no creatures, no buildings in the centre, no text, no border.

PALETTE: #150F1A #1E1626 #2A2036 #3A3050 #5C607A #E2A129
```

## 2️⃣ 白天商店 · `title.day`

```
Pixel-art background illustration, 1800x1300, opaque, 16-bit SNES style.
Hard pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, limited palette.

The inside of a poor woodcutter's cottage at dawn, seen from across the room:
rough timber walls, a cold stone hearth on the left, a small window on the right
with grey morning light coming through, a workbench with tools, bundles of dried

VERY dark and desaturated — a shop panel and two columns of buttons sit on top

No characters, no text, no border, no vignette.

PALETTE: #1A1420 #2A2030 #452D2B #8D5E34 #7B6A56 #E2A129
```

## 3️⃣ 天亮評分 · `title.dawn`

```
Pixel-art background illustration, 1800x1300, opaque, 16-bit SNES style.
Hard pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, limited palette.

Sunrise over a village rooftop line, seen from just above the roofs: chimneys
and thatch in silhouette along the bottom third, a wide pale orange sky above
them, thin mist between the houses, the first light catching the roof edges.

The sky must stay soft and empty — a score panel and six bars are drawn over it.
Low contrast, muted; this is the quiet after a bad night, not a triumphant
sunrise.

No characters, no text, no border.

PALETTE: #1A1420 #452D2B #7B6A56 #E2A129 #FFCC33 #FFF8E6
```

它其實不是純黑；通關才是。優先度低。
