## 🟠 2. 七張場地背景（單項最大的視覺提升）

程式**早就支援**了，只是一直沒有圖 —— 現在七夜全部是同一片黑底加程式畫的
樹。七張背景會讓每一夜看起來像不同的地方。

| 夜 | 場地 | 資源鍵 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 村子廣場 | `ground.village_square` |
| 2 | 磨坊 | `ground.mill` |
| 3 | 林邊 | `ground.forest_edge` |
| 4 | 市集 | `ground.market` |
| 5 | 禮拜堂 | `ground.chapel` |
| 6 | 屠宰場 | `ground.butchery` |
| 7 | 森林深處 | `ground.deep_forest` |

⚠️ 這七張的規格跟角色圖**完全不同**：橫的、鋪滿、不要透明背景、**不要主體**。
它是玩家在上面跑來跑去的地板，主角和怪會蓋在上面。

指令模板（把 `SUBJECT` 那行換掉就好）：

```
Top-down pixel-art game background tile, 1800x1040 landscape, opaque, edge to
edge. 16-bit SNES style: hard pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, limited palette.

This is a FLOOR seen from directly above — a surface characters walk on. It must
read as flat ground, not as a scene with a horizon. No characters, no creatures,
no text, no UI, no vignette, no border, no title.

Very dark and desaturated overall: the game draws it under a lantern that lights
only a small circle, so anything bright competes with the light. Think "almost
black, with just enough texture to tell where you are".

SUBJECT: <換這一行>

PALETTE: #1A1420 #2A2030 #452D2B #231815 #3A3040 #7B6A56
```

七個 SUBJECT：

1. `A village square of packed dirt and worn cobblestones, a dry well off to one side, scattered straw.`
2. `A mill yard of flour-dusted flagstones, spilled grain sacks, the shadow of a great wheel across the ground.`
3. `The edge of a forest — dirt path giving way to moss, roots breaking the surface, fallen leaves.`
4. `An abandoned market — overturned stalls, torn awnings lying flat, spilled fruit rotting on the stones.`
5. `A chapel floor of cracked stone tiles, a faded mosaic in the centre, guttered candle stubs.`
6. `A butchery floor of dark wet stone with drainage channels cut into it, scattered straw, old stains.`
7. `Deep forest floor — thick roots, black soil, pale mushrooms, no path at all.`
```

Pixel-art icon sheet, 4 columns by 2 rows, 1600x800, transparent background.

empty transparency, no frames, no borders, no text, no numbers.

Row 1: a forked lightning bolt (purple #B48CFF) / a radiant sun burst
(pale gold #FAE8A8) / a curling tornado funnel (pale teal #96D6C8) /
a water bubble cage with bars of water (blue #6EA8E8)

Row 2: a crackling armour ring of lightning (violet #C4A8FF) / a shield with a
cross of light on it (cream #FFF4CE) / a running figure made of wind streaks
(mint #B2E8DA) / a cresting tidal wave (deep blue #6096DC)
